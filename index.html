Origin

What "starseed" actually means

The term describes a soul that experiences this life as one stop among many — not human by first nature, but human for now. It surfaced across New Age and channeling communities in the late 20th century, drawing on far older ideas of celestial origin and reincarnation.

Whether you read it as literal cosmology or as a language for a very real feeling of otherness, the pattern it describes shows up consistently: heightened empathy, early disillusionment with conventional paths, and a persistent, low-hum sense of mission.

Starseed Transmission doesn't ask you to believe anything. It asks you to notice what you already feel, and gives it a vocabulary.
